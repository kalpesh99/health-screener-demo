Built to help you understand

The current screeners use CDC BRFSS survey data from 253,680 US adults. Results describe patterns in that dataset, rather than predicting your next ten years.

A starting point, not a diagnosis

Use your result to guide a conversation with a healthcare professional. A low result does not rule out a condition.
